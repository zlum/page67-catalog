Page67 privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Page67 is a book reader for Android. This page explains what the app does with your data. In short: the app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and the developer receives nothing about you or your reading.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in the app is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • the books you add (imported files, the sample books and books downloaded from the free catalogue) and their covers;
  • your reading settings (theme, font, text size, brightness and so on);
  • reading positions, bookmarks, the list of places you jumped from, how much sand you cleared on each page, and the gems you found;
  • whether you donated, and when the app last asked you to.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of it.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may copy part of this data to your backup so it can be restored on a new phone: settings, reading positions, bookmarks, jump places, cleared sand, gems and the donation state. Books, covers and the list of books in your library are never backed up. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account; the developer cannot see it. You can turn backup off in Android settings.

Network requests

The app goes online only for the free book catalogue, and only when you use it:

  • When you open Browse Free Books, the app downloads the catalogue (lists of book titles) from zlum.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages.
  • When you tap a book and confirm the download, the app downloads that book from www.gutenberg.org (Project Gutenberg).

These requests are ordinary HTTPS downloads. They contain no account, device ID or reading data, only the request itself and an app identifier (Page67/<version> (Android; +https://zlum.github.io/page67-catalog/)). Like any web server, those hosts see your IP address and the time of the request. Their own policies apply: GitHub privacy statement and Project Gutenberg privacy policy.

Opening a book file from your phone, reading and digging work offline and send nothing.

Donations

Donations are optional one-time purchases made through Google Play. Payment and billing details are handled entirely by Google under the Google Payments privacy notice. The app only asks Google Play whether a donation exists on this account, so it can say thank you and stop asking; it never sees your name, email or payment method.

Permissions

The app asks for no runtime permissions. It uses internet access (for the catalogue) and Google Play billing (for donations). Files you open come through Android's file picker, "Open with" or "Share", which give the app access to that one file only.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Changes that affect what data leaves your device will also be mentioned in the app's release notes on Google Play.
